# CoSetup Marketplace Taxonomy

Categories are catalogue-specific.

- Application & Scripts has its own category vocabulary.
- Website Templates has its own category vocabulary.
- Industries, Technologies and Product Types are shared.
- Categories support multiple selections.
- A listing should have one primary category and may have additional
  relevant categories.
- Avoid using categories as keywords. Only select categories that
  materially describe the product.
